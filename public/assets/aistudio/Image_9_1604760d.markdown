## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a trustworthy, civic-scale digital infrastructure designed to bridge citizens, grassroots charities, and purpose-driven enterprises. The aesthetic merges institutional credibility with human warmth, moving away from sterile bureaucracy toward transparent, action-oriented civic engagement. 

### Visual Movement: Modern Civic Humanism
The interface blends corporate clarity and modern civic-tech precision. Generous whitespace, structural grid layouts, and structured data tables provide immediate utility and legible metrics. Rounded structural geometry softens the systematic tone, ensuring the platform feels inviting to individual donors and everyday volunteers without diminishing institutional rigor for enterprise auditing.

### Key Personality Attributes
- **Trustworthy & Verifiable:** Clear lines, deliberate data surfaces, and high-contrast validation states project financial-grade transparency and security.
- **Empathetic & Grounded:** Rich forest tones ground the experience, while golden accents highlight community milestones and impact goals.
- **Utilitarian & Legible:** Uncluttered typography hierarchy and high-density data visualizations ensure accessibility across diverse demographics and devices.

## Colors

The palette balances deep forest greens that represent institutional stability and growth with warm golden amber tones denoting energy, generosity, and progress. Slate neutrals provide a clean, high-contrast structural foundation.

### Palette Architecture
- **Primary (`#047857`):** The primary emerald green serves as the foundational interactive color for primary buttons, active states, key navigation indicators, and verified badges.
- **Secondary (`#F59E0B`):** Warm amber is used sparingly to draw focus toward match bonuses, collective milestones, urgent community calls, and progress bars.
- **Tertiary (`#064E3B`):** Deep forest green anchors display headers, high-level dashboard metrics, and secure credential cards, reinforcing longevity and authority.
- **Neutral (`#0F172A`):** Deep slate ensures maximum typographic contrast against pure white (`#FFFFFF`) surfaces and light neutral ground planes (`#F8FAFC`, `#F1F5F9`).
- **Supportive Accents:** Tonal sage (`#ECFDF5`) and mint tints provide soft backgrounds for status indicators, SDG mapping badges, and eligibility tags.

## Typography

The typography system pairs **Plus Jakarta Sans** for prominent, inviting headers with **Inter** for data readability across dashboards, verification tables, and dense metrics.

### Typography Hierarchy Guidelines
- **Headlines & Display:** Plus Jakarta Sans features slightly rounded geometric letterforms that convey open, friendly civic warmth. Large titles maintain a tight negative letter spacing (`-0.01em` to `-0.02em`) to preserve visual density.
- **Body & Data:** Inter provides neutral optical clarity across small labels, tables, and narrative updates. Its consistent x-height ensures sustained readability in low-light environments and accessible contrast levels.
- **Labels & Micro-Tags:** Badges, commitment chips, and SDG indicators use `label-sm` or `label-xs` set in medium or bold weights with uppercase tracking (`+0.02em` to `+0.04em`) to establish crisp hierarchy within compact components.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout operates on an 8-point baseline rhythm designed to enforce consistency across standard dashboards, pledge flows, and initiative registries.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Mobile (< 640px):** 4-column layout with `margin-sm` (16px) and `gutter-sm` (16px). Cards span the full 4 columns; interactive targets must maintain a minimum 44px vertical profile.
- **Tablet (640px – 1024px):** 8-column layout with `margin` (24px) and `gutter` (24px). Accommodates dual-pane views such as search/filter drawers alongside initiative feeds.
- **Desktop (> 1024px):** 12-column layout maxing out at a 1280px content container with `margin-lg` (48px) and `gutter-lg` (32px). Enables asymmetric splits: a 4-column audit summary alongside an 8-column verified timeline.

### Spacing Tokens
- `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px) govern tight component-level architecture: badge paddings, list-item text gaps, and icon-to-label offsets.
- `space-md` (16px) establishes standard card internal padding and input field margins.
- `space-lg` (24px) and `space-xl` (40px) define section stacking distances and structural card gaps.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses a refined tonal layering model supported by soft, low-contrast ambient shadows. The system avoids deep skeuomorphic drop shadows, opting for clean, structural elevation that prioritizes legibility and perceived transparency.

### Layering Philosophy
- **Base Ground (`#F8FAFC`):** The default application canvas.
- **Layer 1 Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** High-level operational cards, tables, and search modules set against the base canvas, defined by a 1px boundary (`#E2E8F0`).
- **Layer 2 Floating Surface (`#FFFFFF`):** Flyouts, dropdown menus, and popovers. These employ subtle ambient shadows tinted with neutral slate: `0 4px 16px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Layer 3 Modals & Overlays:** Critical verification modals and action confirmations rest on a dimmed slate backdrop (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.45)`) paired with `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.12)`.

### Border Accents
Borders function as structural guides. Rather than relying on shadow alone, components like stat-cards and eligibility blocks use a clean 1px border (`#E2E8F0`) that subtly shifts to primary emerald (`#047857`) upon focus or verification confirmation.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a balanced **Rounded (`2`)** profile. This radius reflects approachable modern civic tech: geometric enough to retain systematic institutional order, yet rounded enough to feel accessible.

### Geometry Hierarchy
- **Standard (`0.5rem` / 8px):** Applied to core interactive surfaces including text input fields, standard primary/secondary buttons, list items, and standard cards.
- **Large (`1rem` / 16px):** Applied to primary feature modules, summary containers, analytical dashboards, and modal overlays.
- **Pill (`9999px`):** Reserved exclusively for status markers, SDG alignment tags, match percentage chips, and avatar containers to separate data metadata from interactive buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Solid `#047857` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Height of 44px (desktop) or 48px (mobile), 8px border radius, font `label-md`. Hover state shifts to `#064E3B`; active state reduces opacity to 90%.
- **Secondary:** Clean white `#FFFFFF` surface with a 1px border (`#CBD5E1`) and `#0F172A` text. Hover applies a soft neutral background (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Accent (Milestone / Match Action):** Warm sun amber (`#F59E0B`) with dark slate (`#0F172A`) typography, reserved for high-impact matching flows.

### Badges & Chips
- **Match Percentage Badge:** Pill shape, pale emerald background (`#ECFDF5`), bold emerald text (`#047857`), paired with an icon prefix. Displays verified enterprise match ratios (e.g., "100% Matched").
- **SDG Badges:** Pill shape with an 8px square color swatch reflecting the global SDG color standard, flanked by 11px uppercase `label-xs` typography on an ultra-light slate background (`#F1F5F9`).
- **Commitment Tags:** Soft amber background (`#FEF3C7`) with deep amber text (`#92400E`) used to designate pending commitments or active corporate pledges.

### Input Fields
- Built on a 44px base height with an 8px radius and a 1px border (`#CBD5E1`).
- Focus state removes standard browser outlines in favor of a crisp 2px primary emerald ring (`#047857`) with zero shadow bleed.
- Integrated validation badges indicate whether a charitable entity has passed verified non-profit screening.

### Cards
- Pure white (`#FFFFFF`) background with an 8px or 16px radius, enclosed by a 1px border (`#E2E8F0`).
- Padding is strictly assigned via `space-md` or `space-lg`.
- Impact Cards contain a distinct 3-zone visual structure: top metadata (SDG + Match Badges), center content (headline + partner info), and a bottom-pinned status bar summarizing funds deployed against goals.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Size: 18x18px with a 4px corner radius for checkboxes, circular for radios.
- Unchecked: 1.5px border (`#94A3B8`) on `#FFFFFF`.
- Checked: Solid `#047857` fill featuring a crisp white geometric check or inner dot.

### Specialized Civic Component: Verified Impact Meter
- A horizontal progress bar configured with a two-tone fill: a solid forest emerald segment indicating deployed citizen contributions, joined by an amber segment indicating pledged business match funds.
